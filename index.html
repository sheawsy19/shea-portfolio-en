PERSONAL STRENGTHS

What shapes my practice.

01

Working across disciplines

My practice spans graphic design, video editing, 3D modeling, generative AI, and basic programming. I develop visual and multimedia projects from concept to delivery, building connections across media. My work has received national recognition, including a China Star Design Award Silver Award and a National Third Prize in the National College Students Advertising Art Design Competition.

02

Connecting expression and purpose

I enjoy product and communication design, considering strategy from both market and user perspectives. My planning projects connect user experience with practical feasibility, with recognition from the Academy Award of Advertising Festival of Chinese College Students and the China University Students PR Planning Contest.

03

Bringing people and ideas together

Serving as Head of Publicity and leading teams in innovation competitions and planning projects has developed my ability to coordinate people, communicate clearly, and manage parallel responsibilities. I take ownership of moving collaborative work forward.

04

Learning through making

I approach new tools and unfamiliar fields with openness, actively extending my skills as the discipline evolves. Competitions, coursework, and practical projects help me deepen both my understanding and my ability to execute ideas.
